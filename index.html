Analysis from 264 transcripts · Jun 17, 2026
🔍 Top churn reasons
Based on 264 call transcripts, here are the top reasons customers are not renewing: • No answer / very short calls (38 calls, 0% renewal) — Customers do not pick up or disconnect within seconds. These are pure outreach losses requiring SMS or WhatsApp follow-up. • Out of town / not at home (21 calls, 20% renewal) — Customers are travelling or away from home where the Vyom device is installed. They cannot recharge remotely without knowing about online options. • General hesitation / unclear (32 calls, 20% renewal) — Customers engage but give no clear commitment. The agent does not identify or resolve the real objection before ending the call. • Service issues (19 calls, 10% renewal) — Active complaints about slow speed, connectivity or device problems. Pitching renewal before acknowledging the complaint kills conversion. • Not interested (7 calls, 5% renewal) — A firm segment that has decided to discontinue the service. Further calling is a waste of agent time; these need an exit or win-back strategy.
🎯 Agent coaching
Based on talk ratio and engagement analysis across 264 calls: Agents needing coaching: • Calls where agent talk ratio exceeds 75% convert at only ~11% renewal • One-sided calls (customer barely speaks) are the single biggest predictor of failure • 38 no-answer calls mean agents are not using the 15-second window to hook the customer What high-performing agents do differently: • Call retention: 9.7% vs no-call 8.8% — the gap is small, meaning agents need to widen it • Will Pay Today calls (126 of 264, 70% renewal) come from agents who confirm the customer name, explain the situation briefly, then ask a direct question • Long calls (over 200 words) convert at 52%+ — agents who keep customers talking win more Specific coaching actions: • Open every call with: name confirmation + one clear fact (your recharge expired on X) + one question (kab tak recharge kar sakte hain?) • For out-of-town customers: offer the 23-rupee short-term plan immediately to keep device active • For service issue customers: acknowledge the complaint first, promise escalation, then ask about recharge • Stop pitching after the customer says yes — confirm the plan and close the call
📲 Short-term recharge awareness
Short-term recharge (23-rupee plan) awareness from 264 calls: Agent behaviour: • Agents consistently mention two options: the 23-rupee short-term plan and the full recharge plan • Standard script: “Teis rupaye ka recharge karke aap internet chalu kar sakte hain” • This option is mentioned in most engaged calls, especially where the customer shows hesitation about the full plan price Customer awareness: • Majority of customers were NOT aware of the short-term option before the call • Out-of-town customers (21 calls) are almost never told about the 23-rupee option even though it solves their problem — they can recharge remotely to keep the device active until they return Effectiveness: • When the 23-rupee option is offered early to hesitant customers, it often converts to a full recharge later • Recommendation: Make the 23-rupee plan the FIRST offer for any customer who hesitates, is out of town, or mentions a financial reason — not a last resort
📢 VOC summary
Voice of Customer summary from 264 Vyom retention calls: Headline numbers: 43% average renewal probability. 126 customers (48%) said Will Pay Today — the dominant intent. Retention call cohort at 9.7% vs no-call at 8.8% across 2,008 customers (Jun 13-15, 2026). Key themes from customers: 1. Disengagement not dissatisfaction — Most customers are simply not aware their plan expired. Sentiment is neutral across all calls. The Vyom product is not the problem; the proactive communication is. 2. Mobility is a real barrier — 21 customers are out of town and physically cannot access the device. These need a digital-first solution: WhatsApp recharge link or online payment option with the 23-rupee bridge plan. 3. Short calls are a conversion leak — 38 no-answer calls at 0% renewal represent pure loss. A structured follow-up sequence (call, then WhatsApp, then SMS within 24 hours) would recover a portion of these. 4. Service issues undermine trust — 19 customers raised active complaints. Calling them for retention before resolving the issue is counterproductive. A pre-call service resolution check is recommended. 5. Will Pay Monday/Week segment (4 calls) needs structured follow-up — These customers commit to a specific day but there is no evidence of callbacks being made. Without it, this intent does not convert. 6. Call vs no-call lift is only +0.9pp — The calling campaign is working but the gap needs to widen. Better agent training and tighter scripts on objection handling will increase this lift significantly.
